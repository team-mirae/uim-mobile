uim-mobile layouts

Example drawings of every layout in SPEC.md v0.15. Each board is 13u wide with 6 rows. Keys stretch to fill a row, and hatched areas are reserved: they're drawn but ignore touches.

Try it

The mode keys (S E A a 한 ひ カ, and 한A1 あA1 on the cycle rows), ⇪ and ⇧ work. Character keys type their legend as is. In KO narrow, drag on a key to flick it.

This shows raw key legends only: no Hangul composition, kana conversion or predictions. The candidate bar shows sample content.

Bottom rows

The default row (Idempotent, KO priority) is used in every drawing below. Settings can swap in one of the others for every layout. On the idempotent rows every mode has its own key. On the cycle rows, 한A1 steps KO → EN → symbols and あA1 steps ひ → カ → EN → symbols; from a state outside its cycle, each goes to its first state. On Cycle, A and Cycle, a, the A or a key goes to English, and to the other case if it's already there.
